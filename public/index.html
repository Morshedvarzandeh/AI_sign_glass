    How to try the six handshapes

    Keep your palm facing the camera, fingers upright, and your whole hand in view. Use good lighting. Hold a shape steady until a suggestion appears. For calls, enlarge the signer’s video and keep ClearSign visible beside the call. For recordings, use playback controls to pause or replay. Suggestions can be wrong, including for unsupported signs.

    A
    Closed fist, thumb resting upright beside it.
    B
    Four fingers straight and together, thumb folded across the palm.
    I
    Little finger up; other fingers and thumb folded.
    L
    Index finger up and thumb out, forming an L.
    V
    Index and middle fingers up and apart; thumb folded.
    Y
    Thumb and little finger out; other fingers folded.

    These instructions describe the limited poses this prototype checks. They are not an ASL course. Movement, words, facial grammar, and the other letters are unsupported.
